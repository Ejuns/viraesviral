Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026

This policy explains what data ViraEsviral Publisher (the "Tool") accesses, how it is used and how it is protected. The Tool is a private tool: it is only used with ViraEsviral's own accounts and it does not collect data from other people.

1. Data we access

When one of our accounts is connected through TikTok Login Kit, the Tool accesses only what it needs, through the permissions we grant:

  • Basic account information (user.info.basic): the account's identifier (open ID), username, display name and avatar, to show which account a video will be published to.
  • Publishing (video.publish): the creator settings TikTok requires before posting (available privacy options, comment, duet and stitch settings, maximum video length), and the upload of the videos we approve.
  • Our videos' statistics (video.list): the identifiers, links, publication time and public counters (views, likes, comments and shares) of the videos published by our own accounts.

We do not access followers' personal data, private messages, contacts or any other user's data.

2. How we use it

Only to publish our own approved videos and to measure how they perform, so we can make better videos. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it and we do not share it with third parties.

3. Where it is stored and how it is protected

  • Data is stored on our own computer. Access tokens are kept in files that only the owner can read; they are never stored in the database, in source code or in any shared location.
  • Connections to TikTok use HTTPS.

4. Retention and deletion

Video statistics are kept while they are useful to measure performance. When an account is disconnected, its access tokens are revoked and deleted and its videos are no longer measured. To request the deletion of any data, write to the contact address below.

5. Children

The Tool is not directed to children and is not used to collect data from them.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows the latest version.
