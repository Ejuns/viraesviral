Short videos that explain things clearly

ViraEsviral publishes short educational videos in Spanish about personal finance, business, careers and productivity, applied AI, technology and cybersecurity. Each video explains one idea with a concrete example and real numbers, and cites its sources.

ViraEsviral Publisher

ViraEsviral Publisher is the private tool we use to publish our own videos to our own accounts on TikTok and other platforms. Every video is reviewed and approved by a person before it is published, and videos made with the help of AI are labelled as AI-generated content.

The tool is not offered to the public: only ViraEsviral's own accounts are connected to it.
